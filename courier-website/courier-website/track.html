FREQUENTLY ASKED

Tracking FAQs

Where do I find my tracking ID?
Your tracking ID is sent via SMS and email immediately after booking. It starts with "SD-" followed by 6 digits.
How often is tracking updated?
Tracking updates are pushed every 30 minutes at key checkpoints, and in real-time when out for delivery.
My parcel shows "Delayed" — what should I do?
Delays are rare but can occur due to weather, high volume, or address issues. Contact our support team with your tracking ID for immediate assistance.
Can I change the delivery address after shipping?
Address changes are possible if the parcel has not yet left the origin hub. Call us at +91 98765 43210 immediately after booking.
